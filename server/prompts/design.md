Make the specified metal look cast and carefully finished, with consistent clean top faces, fine edge highlights and restrained physical depth. Treatment judgment: sculpted bas relief has modeled solid-metal forms; photo relief retains photographic detail in shallow metal tones; etched photography stays flat and tonal; full-color UV remains flat, natural-color photography. These are separate processes. Never turn a photo-relief order into a sculpted bust or tint UV artwork bronze. A flat tonal photo in an example does not establish its manufacturing process; follow the catalog treatment reference.
